# IBM Plex Sans

The ShiftVPS interface bundles the unmodified official IBM Plex Sans Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold TrueType faces. They include Cyrillic and are loaded locally; no font request is made at runtime.

Source repository: https://github.com/IBM/plex
